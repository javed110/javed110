# Javed Ashraf

I work on health data science, oral health and epidemiology, with an emphasis on reproducible analysis, uncertainty and responsible data use. My methods include Bayesian modeling, observational-study analysis and causal-method benchmarking in Python and R.

Explore the [health data science portfolio](https://github.com/javed110/health-data-science-portfolio) for project context and methods.

## Research and demonstration materials

- [Dental AI survey in Pakistan](https://github.com/javed110/dental-ai-survey-pakistan): R code and aggregate results for a cross-sectional survey. The manuscript is in preparation; associations do not establish causality. Respondent-level data are withheld, and full analysis reproduction requires an authorized local data package.
- [Parvovirus B19 causal-method benchmark](https://github.com/javed110/parvovirus-b19-causal-ml-benchmark): known-truth simulations and an aggregate data-quality audit. The manuscript is prepared for submission and is not peer reviewed. Simulation results do not establish clinical treatment effects; patient-level source and synthetic records are withheld.
- [SehatAman PCC 2026 reviewer materials](https://github.com/javed110/sehataman-pcc-2026-reviewer): public source, a contact-redacted concept note, recordings and engineering evidence for a synthetic local MVP of patient-controlled diagnostic-report sharing. The app is not deployed or clinically validated. Direct stakeholder and independent/domain validation remain pending.
